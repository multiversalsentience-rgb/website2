The bridge to Earth

Making an Avatar on a new planet is just the start.

The Avatar success stories explore embodiment, identity, AI, sub-spatial communication, and the tragicomic task of putting Multiversal perception into other sapients' syntax in ways that actually work.

They also give readers a grounded human entry point. The stakes are enormous for humanity, yes. But, its good to see how other species have succeeded.
